BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER - install on Mac
=====================

1. Copy "BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER.vst3"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   Copy "BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/

2. Open Terminal and remove the download quarantine:
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER.vst3
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER.component

3. In Ableton: Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan.
   BLEEP-BLOP-VOCAL-ICER shows up under Plug-ins > Sunrise Sound.
